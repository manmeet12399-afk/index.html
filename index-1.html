Before you drop

Drop rules

Real, active communities only

The invite must work, lead to the server you describe and not expire. Empty or abandoned servers are removed.

Keep it safe

No hate, harassment, scams, doxxing, account trading or anything illegal. Servers built around that are removed on sight.

No adult servers

Servers centred on NSFW content are not listed. Keep the description clean as well.

Your servers, your control

Add every server you own or help run. Only you can edit or delete your listings, and they go live as soon as you publish.

We can remove any listing

Because drops go live without review, staff check the directory afterwards and remove anything that breaks the rules, for example a dead invite or an unsafe server.
